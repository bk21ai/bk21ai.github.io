제주에서 열리는 인공지능의 만남

An AI Gathering on Jeju Island

제6회 한동대 BK21 인공지능 워크샵에 여러분을 초대합니다.

인공지능은 이미 우리 사회 전반의 혁신을 이끄는 핵심 기술로 자리 잡았으며, 최근에는 추론 능력을 갖춘 거대언어모델(Large Language Models, LLMs), 자율적으로 과업을 수행하는 Agentic AI, 그리고 물리 세계와 상호작용하는 Physical AI로 그 지평을 빠르게 넓혀가고 있습니다.

이러한 흐름 속에서 한동대 BK21 AI 혁신인재 양성사업단은 협력 기업 및 대학의 연구자분들, 그리고 학생 여러분께 AI의 최신 기술 동향과 산업계 활용 사례를 함께 나누고자 본 워크샵을 마련하였습니다. 본 워크샵에서는 한동대 BK21 AI 혁신인재 양성사업단 소속 교수님들께서 사업단을 통해 수행해 온 AI 연구 성과와 주요 기술 동향을 소개해 드리고, 이어서 선도 연구자 초청 강연과 산업계 리더 특강을 통해 학계와 산업 현장의 생생한 통찰을 함께 나누는 자리를 마련하였습니다.

참석하시는 모든 분들께 의미 있고 유익한 시간이 되리라 확신하며, 학계 및 산업계의 AI 연구자와 대학·대학원생 여러분의 많은 참여를 진심으로 바랍니다.

— 한동대학교 BK21 인공지능사업단

We invite you to the 6th Handong BK21 AI Workshop.

Artificial intelligence has firmly established itself as a core technology driving innovation across society, and its frontiers are rapidly expanding — toward Large Language Models (LLMs) with reasoning capabilities, Agentic AI that autonomously carries out tasks, and Physical AI that interacts with the physical world.

Amid these developments, the Handong BK21 AI Innovative Talent Education & Research Group has prepared this workshop to share the latest technological trends and industry use cases of AI with researchers from partner companies and universities, and with students. Professors of the group will first present the AI research achievements and major technological trends they have pursued through the program, followed by an invited keynote from a leading researcher and tech talks offering vivid insights from both academia and industry.

We are confident this will be a meaningful and rewarding time for everyone, and we sincerely welcome the participation of AI researchers in academia and industry, as well as undergraduate and graduate students.

— Handong Global University BK21 AI Education & Research Group

Prof. Kijung Shin
KAIST
AI for Complex Networks: With a Focus on Hypergraphs
Talk Overview

AI for complex networks supports a wide range of applications, including web search, recommendation, advertising, and fraud detection, across the web, online social networks, and e-commerce platforms. However, it also poses unique challenges due to the massive scale but limited number of such networks, as well as their structural properties, such as permutation invariance. In this seminar, I will present our recent research on AI for complex networks, focusing on hypergraphs that represent interconnected group interactions. The seminar will cover embedding, generation, and modeling of large-scale group interactions. I will also briefly discuss broader directions, including complex networks for AI and AI for complex systems beyond networks.

Bio

Kijung Shin is an Associate Professor in the Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI at KAIST. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Carnegie Mellon University in 2019 and his B.Eng. in Computer Science and Engineering from Seoul National University in 2015. His primary research interests include complex networks, graph algorithms, and data mining. He has published more than 100 peer-reviewed papers in top-tier AI and data-related venues, and his work has been recognized with the IEEE ICDM Best Paper Award in 2025, the IEEE ICDM Best Student Paper Runner-up Award in 2023, and the ACM SIGKDD Best Research Paper Award in 2016. He also received the KIISE/IEEE Young Computer Researcher Award in 2025 and the Songam Distinguished Research Award in 2022.

Advancing Multimodal Medical AI through Global Research Collaboration
Talk Overview

Multimodal medical AI has become an increasingly important research direction as clinical applications require the integration of diverse data sources, including medical images, electronic health records, physiological signals, and text. In this talk, I will share our three-year collaborative research experience with Prof. Yuyin Zhou at the University of California, Santa Cruz, supported by the IITP Global Research Support Program in Digital Fields. Each year, two Handong graduate students have visited the UCSC Computer Science Department as visiting researchers for four to six months. I will introduce the research outcomes from this collaboration, including papers, abstracts, domestic publications, and media coverage, with several results presented or submitted to top-tier international venues in medical AI, machine learning, and neuroscience. I will also discuss how this collaboration has contributed to student growth, research direction, and future opportunities in multimodal medical AI.

Bio

Charmgil Hong is an Associate Professor in the School of Artificial Intelligence, Computer and Electrical Engineering at Handong Global University. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Pittsburgh in 2017. His recent research focuses on multimodal and multi-source machine learning for complex real-world data, including time-series, image, video, and text data. His work connects anomaly and outlier detection, video understanding, large-scale video indexing, LLM-based discourse analysis, and AI-driven decision support, with applications in medical and industrial domains. His work has been recognized with the IEEE AVSS 2024 Best Paper Award, the AAAI Doctoral Consortium Grant, and travel grants from NSF, SIAM, and ICML. He currently serves as a technical advisor to several AI and healthcare technology companies, including Impactive AI, Claimark, AI Cloud, Domingo Research, and Herings.
